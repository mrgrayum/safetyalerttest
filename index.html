Know Your Controls

Preventative or mitigative?

A preventative control stops the unwanted event from happening. A mitigative control limits the damage once it does. Sort each one.

Card 1 of 7

Preventative controls

  • Ensure an approved grounding plan is in place, communicated, and utilized.
  • Position spotters on the ground so they can visibly observe workers in the air at all times.
  • Install temporary protective grounds at approved connection points on the conductor — never on the dead-end assembly itself.
  • Physically inspect previously installed grounds to confirm they're tight and secure before work proceeds.
  • Install and secure temporary grounds so they can't be inadvertently contacted or interfered with — relocate using approved methods if they do.

Mitigative controls

  • Establish and verify a rescue plan that can be implemented quickly.
  • Make sure an AED is readily available.
